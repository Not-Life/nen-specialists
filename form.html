Design a Specialist

Help make the rarest Nen type in a Hunter x Hunter mod for Slay the Spire 2.

The setup

You start every run as a new Nen user

A Water Divination test at the start of the run decides your aura type. Specialists are the rare result: their abilities break the normal rules. In the show that's Chrollo stealing abilities, Neon's fortune telling, and Kurapika's Emperor Time.

  • 念Nen is a resource you build up and spend on abilities. Energy is your physical stamina.
  • 誓Limitations and vows make abilities stronger in exchange for a rule or a risk.
  • 特Your idea doesn't need to be from the show. Anything you'd think is cool works: from another anime, a game, a myth, or your own head.

Takes about 5 minutes. Rough ideas are great.
