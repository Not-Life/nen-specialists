The Hunter

A Slay the Spire 2 character inspired by Hunter x Hunter. You play an original Hunter who becomes a Nen user, and a Water Divination at the start of each run decides what kind.

Who the Hunter is

The character

Not Gon or any other character from the show. The Hunter is your own Hunter, learning Nen from scratch. Cards are inspired by the show's ideas and techniques without being any one character's move.

Anime-only: nothing here draws on manga content.

Start of the run

You begin with the Empty Glass. A Water Divination event, replacing Neow, turns it into one of six aura relics at random. Becoming a Specialist is rare, and a later event can also make you one.

Two resources

Energy is physical stamina. Nen is aura, a second resource built on the Regent's Stars and renamed. Cards can cost energy, Nen, or both.

Every aura relic gives 3 Nen at the start of combat and 1 Nen at the end of each turn.

The four principles

Ren turns aura up, Ten holds it in, Zetsu shuts it off, and Hatsu uses it. Ren, Ten and Zetsu are starter cards that build Nen. Hatsu becomes your aura type's own basic ability.

Card rewards

Your aura relic replaces the normal card reward with a pick of 1 from 3. The pool is your type's normal cards and signatures plus both neighbors' normal cards. Signatures never leave their type. Rares come from elites and bosses.

Conjuration and Manipulation swap their Specialization neighbor for Specialist cards of their own. Specialists only draw their own deck and pull other cards through their own mechanic.

Limitations and vows

At rest sites you can make a vow: a card gets a vow enchantment that makes it stronger with a built-in downside, like losing HP when played or adding a curse to your deck. Vow options live on your aura relic.
